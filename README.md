# 🖼️ ChainCart — Blockchain-Based Digital Art Marketplace  

> *Empowering artists through blockchain transparency and perpetual royalties.*

**ChainCart** is a blockchain-powered digital art marketplace where **artists can list and sell artworks**, and **buyers can purchase them securely**.  
All transactions and ownership transfers are recorded on the **blockchain**, ensuring **transparency**, **security**, and a **10% royalty** to the original artist on every resale.

---

## 🚀 Features  

- 🎨 Artists can upload and list artworks for sale.  
- 💸 Automatic **10% royalty** for artists on every resale.  
- 🔗 Blockchain-based ownership tracking via **Web3.py + Solidity**.  
- 🛍️ Buyers can view, add to cart, and purchase artworks.  
- 🧾 All data stored securely in **MongoDB** and verified on blockchain.
